The [tour](/docs/tour/) shows the language through working programs. These pages
are the same material stated exactly, for looking things up rather than for
reading through.

W# is Zig-flavoured, with one deliberate departure: **type annotations are
optional everywhere.** They are checked when written and inferred when not.
